Our perception of harmony depends on motion between chords—the interval and qualities that give each transition its unique emotional fingerprint. Chordex maps the entire tertian harmonic landscape in relative terms: 192 possible chord-to-chord movements. Each relationship carries its own emotional color, from the spicy tension of M ♭II M to the melancholic yearning of m VII M.

They are cataloged with affect tags, scale associations, and audio playback. String them together like lego bricks to build progressions, and add personal notes as you build your vocabulary. 

A Pokédex for Chord Relationships: for improvisers and composers who think in harmonic gestures.
