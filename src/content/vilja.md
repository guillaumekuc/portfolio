**Melodurgy: mind control through music.**

A city-state is held by a powerful melodurge, who controls its citizens through a dense acoustic network. Her peers — an exile and a dissident — join forces to hold her to account and break her grip.

**Musicianship is the combat system.** Vilja is a music-driven arena shooter: the player frees the subjugated who assail them by playing the right intervals and chords, pushing through the city to reach its ruler.

**A pedagogical Trojan horse.** Players come for the world-building, the story and the arcade action; they leave with a trained ear and an intuitive grasp of harmony.

Self-initiated game project in development, bringing together 2D, 3D, music and code.
