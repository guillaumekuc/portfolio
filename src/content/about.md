# Bonjour !

I'm a technical artist working at the intersection of digital creation and software development. My practice spans 2D and 3D digital arts, music composition, and tools development, with each discipline feeding into the others as part of a unified, mutually reinforcing system.

> **Technical Artist**: A hybrid role combining artistic fluency with programming skills to create tools, workflows, and technical solutions that support creative production.

## Journey

My formal training began at [École Estienne](https://www.ecole-estienne.paris/ecole/presentation/) in Paris, where I studied [3D animation](https://www.ecole-estienne.paris/formation/presentation/#:~:text=Qu'est%2Dce%20que%20le,%C3%A0%20une%20cam%C3%A9ra%20virtuelle%20mobile.) following a scientific baccalaureate. After graduation, I freelanced as a digital painter for RPG publishers and novelists while continually pursuing self-directed learning.

This path led to independent publishing: an educational e-book on applied music theory (_All-fourths Tuning for Guitar_) and a Photoshop plugin (_Gridz_) for generating dynamic perspective grids through trigonometric calculations. These projects revealed that I was gravitating toward the meta-layer — building tools that make creation more fluid — just as much as toward the creative output itself.

My most recent position at [Visioglobe](https://visioglobe.com/) crystallized this dual identity. The production team and I built interactive 3D indoor maps for large enterprise clients including Orange, L'Oréal, Ikea, SG, Unibail-Rodamco-Westfield. In parallel, I took on the initiative of developing workflow plugins and scripts over our proprietary codebase to improve process efficiency, confirming that my trajectory has organically settled at the interface between artistic production and technical infrastructure.

## Current Focus

I am currently investing a season into deepening my technical art direction and probing game and real-time pipelines: procedural modeling in Houdini, game engine workflows and shader development. This exploration aims to open new ways for me to contribute to the French creative ecosystem.
